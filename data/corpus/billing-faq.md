# Billing FAQ

## Plans and pricing
The Free plan costs nothing and includes 10,000 API calls per month. Pro costs $49 per month per project and includes 1 million calls. Enterprise pricing is negotiated per contract.

## Invoicing
Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing cycle and are payable within 30 days. Usage above the included allowance is billed as overage at $0.04 per 1,000 calls on Pro.

## Refunds
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions are not refunded but can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the cycle.

## Plan changes
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remaining days. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Failed payments
When a card payment fails we retry on day 1, day 3 and day 7. After the final failure the project is moved to read-only mode, and it is suspended after 14 days. Updating the payment method clears the failure state.

## Taxes
Sales tax or VAT is added to invoices where applicable. Customers can add a tax identifier in the billing settings.
